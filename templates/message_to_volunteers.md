# Message to send volunteers (copy into WhatsApp)

Hello! We're building an AI model that can tell which Nigerian language someone is speaking, for the DSN 2026 AI competition. Could you help with 3–5 minutes of voice notes in **[LANGUAGE]**?

1. Speak **only [LANGUAGE]**, with no English mixed in. Talk naturally, for example about your day, a story from home, how to cook something you like, or directions to your house.
2. Send a few voice notes of 30 s to 2 min each. Recording in different places is fine: indoors, outside, with some background noise.
3. Reply with your first name, language, gender, age range (18–25, 26–35, 36–50, 50+) and home town or LGA.
4. Reply with the consent line below.

> "I agree that my voice recordings may be used to train and publish a language-identification model for the DSN 2026 competition. I am 18 or older."

Your recordings stay private to our team; only the trained model is published. Thank you!

---

**Priority languages:** Nupe, Igala, Ibibio, Efik and Tiv are the most needed, then Annang, Eggon, Idoma and Urhobo. More **different people** matters more than more minutes from one person.
