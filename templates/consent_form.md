# Voice recording consent (DSN 2026 language-ID model)

Read this to the volunteer, or send it to them, before they record. Keep the signed or replied copy **outside this repo**, in the team's private consent folder.

---

**What this is for.** We are building a computer model that recognises which Nigerian language is being spoken. It is for the Data Science Nigeria (DSN) 2026 AI Bootcamp competition.

**What we ask.** About 3–5 minutes of you speaking naturally in your language. You can send it as WhatsApp voice notes or phone recordings.

**How your recordings are used:**
- Your recordings are used only to train and test this language-identification model.
- The trained model will be published publicly on Hugging Face, as the competition requires.
- The model does not contain your recordings, but it learns from them.
- Your recordings themselves will **not** be published or shared outside our team.
- Your name and contact details are kept separately from the audio, and will **not** be published.
- The model card will say that we used volunteer recordings, with the language and the number of speakers only.

**Your choice.** Taking part is voluntary. You can ask us to delete your recordings at any time before the competition ends on 22 October 2026; after that they will already be part of the trained model.

**To agree, reply with:**

> "I agree that my voice recordings may be used to train and publish a language-identification model for the DSN 2026 competition. I am 18 or older."
